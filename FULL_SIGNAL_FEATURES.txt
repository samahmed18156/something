This package contains the restored clear UI plus the full signal feature.

Full signal fields included in app.py:
- Direction: LONG, SHORT, or WAIT
- Entry price
- Take-profit price
- Stop-loss price
- Direction, Entry, TP, and SL columns in the market overview
- Positive / LONG plan
- Negative / SHORT plan

The entry is the latest completed candle close. TP and SL are ATR-based reference levels.
